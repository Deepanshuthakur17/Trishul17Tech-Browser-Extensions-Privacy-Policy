# Privacy Policy for Multi Links Open

**Effective Date:** November 7, 2026\
**Last Updated:** November 7, 2026

Multi Links Open is a browser extension developed by **Trishul17Tech**.
This Privacy Policy explains what information the extension handles, how
that information is used, and what happens when you use the extension.

## 1. Privacy at a Glance

Multi Links Open helps users select and manage multiple hyperlinks on a
webpage.

-   The extension processes hyperlinks selected by the user to provide
    its link-management features.
-   Selected links may be copied to the clipboard or opened in browser
    tabs, depending on the user's action.
-   The extension may use browser extension storage for settings and
    preferences.
-   The extension does not require an account for its core
    link-selection functionality.
-   This policy does not assume that all processing is local or that no
    data is transmitted. The implementation of the published version
    must be checked before making those claims.

## 2. Information Multi Links Open Handles

### 2.1 Selected Links and Webpage Information

When a user activates the extension and selects hyperlinks, Multi Links
Open accesses the relevant webpage elements and URLs needed to provide
its functionality.

This information may include: - URLs of hyperlinks selected by the
user. - Link information needed to identify and display selected
links. - The number of links selected during an operation.

The extension uses this information to support the user's requested
actions, such as copying selected URLs or opening selected links.

### 2.2 Preferences and Settings

The extension may store configuration preferences required for its
features, such as link-opening options, selection limits, or
duplicate-handling preferences, where those features are implemented.

The exact information stored depends on the installed version and its
enabled functionality.

### 2.3 Information Transmitted Externally

The extension's handling of external network requests must be assessed
against the production package.

Do not assume that information is transmitted or that no information is
transmitted without checking the extension's source code, dependencies,
and network activity.

If the extension sends information to an external service, the relevant
information, purpose, and service must be disclosed here before
publication.

### 2.4 Personal Information

Multi Links Open's core purpose is link selection and management. It is
not designed to request users' names, postal addresses, payment
information, or account passwords.

Users should avoid selecting or sharing URLs that contain private or
sensitive information.

## 3. Webpage Access and Browser Permissions

Multi Links Open requests browser permissions needed to provide its
link-selection and management features.

Depending on the permissions included in the published package, these
may include:

-   **activeTab:** Provides temporary access to the active webpage after
    the user invokes the extension.
-   **scripting:** Allows the extension to run its selection
    functionality on the active webpage.
-   **tabs:** Allows the extension to open selected links in browser
    tabs.
-   **storage:** Allows the extension to save supported settings and
    preferences.
-   **contextMenus:** Allows the extension to provide supported actions
    through the browser's context menu.

Only permissions actually included in the published manifest should be
considered applicable. Any additional host permissions must also be
explained according to their actual purpose.

These permissions must only be used to support the extension's stated
functionality.

## 4. Local Storage and Preferences

Multi Links Open may use browser extension storage to retain user
preferences and settings.

The specific storage API and information retained depend on the
implementation in the published version.

Users can review browser extension settings and remove the extension or
clear its stored data through the browser's available controls.

## 5. Data Collection

Multi Links Open is designed to help users manage multiple hyperlinks.

Trishul17Tech does not claim to collect or retain information beyond

## 10. User Choices and Controls

Users can:

-   Choose which links to select.
-   Choose when to copy selected URLs.
-   Choose when to open selected links.
-   Adjust supported extension settings.
-   Disable or uninstall the extension through their browser.

Available controls may vary by extension version.
