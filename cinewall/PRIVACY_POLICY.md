# Privacy Policy for CineWall for YouTube

**Effective Date:** October 9, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 9, 2026

CineWall for YouTube is a browser extension developed by **Trishul17Tech**. This Privacy Policy explains what information CineWall handles, how that information is used, and what happens when you use the extension.

## 1. Privacy at a Glance

CineWall for YouTube follows a **local-first** architecture designed to enhance your video viewing experience directly within your browser.

- Page styling, theater mode adjustments, and ambient visual enhancements are processed locally in your browser.
- User settings and customization preferences are stored locally on your device.
- CineWall does not operate a developer-owned backend server for collecting browsing activity, viewing history, or user data.
- CineWall does not require account creation, user registration, or authentication credentials.
- CineWall does not intentionally collect personal information such as names, email addresses, payment details, or device identifiers.
- CineWall does not include developer-owned analytics, telemetry, or advertising frameworks.

## 2. Information CineWall Handles

### Information Processed Locally

When you navigate to supported YouTube pages, CineWall interacts locally with the Document Object Model (DOM) and video player elements in your browser to render ambient lighting effects, cinematic backdrops, and player interface adjustments. All visual rendering and canvas manipulations occur on your local machine within the active browser session.

### Preferences and Settings Stored Locally

CineWall saves your extension preferences locally using browser extension storage. Stored data is limited to user configuration choices, including:

- Ambient lighting intensity, blur, spread, and glow parameters
- Active display presets and custom themes
- Toggle states (e.g., enabled/disabled status for specific display modes)
- Performance and rendering preferences

### Information Transmitted Externally

CineWall does not transmit your extension settings, viewing activity, or browsing behavior to any external server or developer-controlled database.

### Information Not Collected

CineWall does not collect or track:

- Personal identification information (names, email addresses, phone numbers)
- Google or YouTube account credentials or profile data
- Video watch history, search queries, playlist contents, or liked videos
- Payment, billing, or financial information
- IP addresses or persistent device identifiers collected by Trishul17Tech

## 3. YouTube Page Access

CineWall requires access to supported YouTube pages (`https://www.youtube.com/*`) because its core functionality enhances the YouTube viewing environment.

To apply visual backdrops and adapt to dynamic video player states, CineWall reads player dimensions and video playback elements on the active page. CineWall does not claim ownership of, intercept, harvest, or store YouTube video content, user interactions, comments, or account information.

## 4. Local Storage and Preferences

CineWall utilizes standard browser extension storage APIs (`chrome.storage.local`) exclusively to save your customized preferences and toggle options. 

This data remains strictly on your local device and is not synchronized with any external Trishul17Tech server.

## 5. Data Collection

Trishul17Tech does not collect, harvest, or log data regarding your browsing habits or your interaction with YouTube videos through CineWall. The extension functions without transmitting operational logs to the developer.

## 6. Data Sharing

CineWall does not sell, rent, trade, or share user data. Specifically:

- **Trishul17Tech:** Does not receive or store your browsing or viewing information.
- **Third-Party Service Providers:** No third-party data processors or storage services are integrated.
- **Analytics Providers:** No analytics or tracking libraries are bundled with or used by CineWall.
- **Advertising Providers:** No advertising networks or targeted marketing trackers are present.
- **YouTube / Google:** Your browser interacts directly with YouTube as part of normal web browsing. CineWall does not act as an intermediary or proxy for your web traffic.

## 7. External Services

CineWall does not communicate with developer-controlled external APIs, remote servers, or external databases during operation. All scripts and assets necessary for CineWall's operation are bundled directly within the extension package.

## 8. Advertising and Analytics

CineWall does not contain advertisements, sponsored placements, affiliate links, or third-party telemetry mechanisms.

## 9. Data Security

CineWall minimizes security risks by executing operations locally within the browser sandbox and eliminating unnecessary data collection and transmission. While Trishul17Tech employs modern best practices to ensure extension safety, users should recognize that no software application or browser environment is immune to all security vulnerabilities.

## 10. User Choices and Controls

You retain full control over your extension configuration and data:

- You can enable, disable, or adjust visual effects at any time via the CineWall popup interface or settings panel.
- You can reset all preferences to their default states directly from the extension settings.
- You can remove all locally stored configuration at any time by clearing extension data in your browser settings or by uninstalling the extension.

## 11. Data Retention

Locally stored preferences remain in browser extension storage until you explicitly reset the settings, clear your browser extension storage, or uninstall CineWall. Trishul17Tech does not maintain any backup or developer-hosted copy of your preferences.

## 12. Children's Privacy

CineWall for YouTube is a general-audience display customization utility and is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the applicable legal age in your jurisdiction). CineWall does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes in extension features, browser platform requirements, or applicable regulations. When updates occur, the **Last Updated** date at the top of this document will be revised accordingly.
